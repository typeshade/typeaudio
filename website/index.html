02 / UNDER THE HOOD
A voice is a class.
A bank is the instrument.
TypeAudio owns the audio semantics. TypeShade compiles the kernels and runs them on the selected backend. The playground uses the actual engine in this repository.
Read the design →
  1. 01
    Compose the voice
    A sawtooth oscillator feeds a lowpass filter. Each voice keeps its own state.
  2. 02
    Render in blocks
    Mix the bank, apply soft clipping and gain, then measure the peak. One fuse plan per block.
  3. 03
    Listen and inspect
    Web Audio plays the rendered samples. The report shows where the kernels ran.
