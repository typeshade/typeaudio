# Sonic direction and full-scene design

Status: authored creative decisions for review. They are not observations of the original soundtrack.
The 41 region IDs and picture evidence come from [spotting.json](../spotting.json).

## Dramatic intention

The scene should move from readable magical combat to loss of control, then regain clarity.
The unseen force gains power from withheld preparation. The final beam gains scale from duration and the room's response.
The closing dissolution must release tension without suggesting another attack.

The intended listener is near the action, with selective changes in attention rather than literal camera-mounted hearing.
Preserve one hall across cuts. Let source detail, distance and density explain changes in perspective.
These decisions are a new effects interpretation. They do not imitate a measured reference sound palette.

## Material grammar

Each recurring source needs an identity that survives pitch, duration and seed changes.
Use a small set of recognizable materials. Reserve the sharpest transients for decisive changes of state.

## Foreground hierarchy

1. Put the action that changes the story in the foreground.
2. Use body and motion layers to explain its force and trajectory.
3. Let a few contacts establish the material and location.
4. Keep fine debris and room tone below those explanations.
5. Lower density before the next important action instead of continuously increasing level.

An extra visible fragment does not require an extra sound. It must contribute scale, timing or narrative information.
Low-frequency energy alone cannot provide scale on a phone. Retain useful harmonics and contact detail above 150 Hz.
Do not manufacture an audible rear or overhead location from a pan curve.

No candidate is selected automatically.

## Space and mix

The current room is an authored rectangular proxy, 18 × 12 × 20 metres, with a 2.1-second artistic decay setting.
It does not reconstruct the circular hall or its columns. These dimensions are creative controls, not measurements.
Every bus uses the same room definition. Linear bus outputs combine into the mix; history persists across events.
The current renderer provides stereo distance damping, six first-order reflections and a diffuse tail.

Source trajectories are authored in metres independently of picture coordinates.
Direct sound remains immediate for sync; reflections use excess path delay.
The 0.750750-second force-to-column interval belongs to the edited action, not an acoustic propagation calculation.

Six editable bus names are available: `space`, `energy`, `movement`, `impact`, `detail` and `debris`.
Keep source envelopes and spatial sends separate. Stronger room send must not automatically raise the direct impact.
Preserve the force scene's low background and the finale's closing reduction during delivery.
Retain midrange room texture during the quiet passage. A sub-only bed can disappear on a phone.
The draft's pre-force background measured about −64 dBFS RMS and was revised before publication.
The revised authoring target is −50 to −35 dBFS RMS for that window, with a 16–30 dB force contrast.
Those targets are production checks, not a substitute for an audibility or comfort review on a physical phone.

The delivery pass applies a 27 Hz highpass and static gain to a complete candidate.
It matches each A/B pair at the highest attainable level at or below −20 LUFS, with 2.5 dB true-peak headroom.
No per-cue normalization, limiter or compressor changes the authored hierarchy.
AAC decode is checked independently because encoding can increase peaks.

These are effects-and-ambience studies. The original picture soundtrack combines dialogue, music and effects.
Muting YouTube removes all three. Isolated dialogue and music are unavailable, so masking with those stems remains unreviewed.
Do not infer speech processing from the numerical mixed-reference measurements.

## Production decisions after listening

Review timing, material, hierarchy, perspective and fatigue separately, at a comfortable fixed playback level.
Use headphones, a phone speaker and mono fold-down. Check the same candidate before changing a parameter.
Record picture time, candidate, device, observation, requested change and reviewer identity.
Make one meaningful revision, rerender, then repeat the same comparison.

Expand the selected materials across the unrendered regions only after this review.
Next unresolved designs are violet branching, dark wedges, multicolour assembly and natural movement detail.
The full 189.314625-second mix, isolated-dialogue premix and editor playback synchronization remain unfinished.

## Requirements this design gives the engine and editor

| Observed production need | Current study method | Required product contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One event contains several causally ordered layers | Absolute sample starts in the offline renderer | Grouped event scheduling, with offsets preserved during edits and undo |
| One material survives several shots | One continuous graph instance per source layer | Stable source identity, parameter automation and explicit retrigger semantics |
| Contact and room response remain independently editable | Per-layer direct placement and scaled reflection/late targets | Separate direct, early and late controls without resetting room history |
| Important transients remain readable when density rises | Authored source density and gains | Priority-aware voice budgets; preserve important attacks and already-playing tails |
| A/B revisions must be attributable | One changed parameter, fixed seeds and source hashes | Versioned recipes, cache invalidation and comparable passage renders |
| An agent must avoid claiming success from meters | Explicit signal checks and pending listening review | Separate rendered, technically verified, reviewed and selected states |
| The Web project must reflect source edits | Source-only graphs plus labelled cached audio | Linked source/render revisions; no stale PCM silently overriding a changed graph |

TypeShade compiles the scalar source programs. TypeAudio defines source lifecycle, routing and space.
Web must preserve those identities through editing, preview, undo and review.
The offline host currently schedules and mixes these studies. It does not prove that those product contracts are shipped.
R1 still needs the bounded real-time source-plus-room contract and physical-device evidence.
